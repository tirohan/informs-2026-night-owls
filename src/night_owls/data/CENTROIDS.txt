County internal points for the 302 challenge counties (IN, OH, PA, WV).

Source: US Census Bureau Gazetteer national county file (Gaz_counties_national.zip),
internal point latitude/longitude (INTPTLAT, INTPTLONG). All 302 FIPS codes in
DM_Train.csv and DM_Test.csv matched. These coordinates are static geography.
They are not model labels and do not depend on the storm window.

The loader uses fips, latitude, and longitude only.
